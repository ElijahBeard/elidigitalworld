## Design Ethos (annoying)

elidigitalworld.xyz is a simple personal webpage. It's a personal portfolio, and sandbox for web development. 

Working against the current trend of centralized web-app development is the goal.

Over-use and dependance on companies like Google, Amazon and Facebook for web dev will ultimately be the demise of countless websites. Everyone in the world doesn't need to use AWS for their personal projects. 

Sure, you could decimate a small town's local energy and water resources for your epic OpenAI powered webapp that combines the world of AI, the NBA and the block-chain. Or, you could put your thinking cap on and learn this all works, get knees deep in networking and have a more satisfying experience.
